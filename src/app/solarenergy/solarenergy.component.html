SOLAR ENERGY

Solar energy is a type of renewable energy that comes from the sun in the form of light, heat, and ultraviolet rays. Solar energy can be used to generate electricity or capture thermal energy. Solar energy is the energy derived from the Sun's radiation. It can be harnessed in two primary forms: solar photovoltaic (PV) energy (to generate electricity) and solar thermal energy (to capture heat). Solar energy is a clean, renewable resource that has the potential to power homes, industries, and entire cities while reducing dependence on fossil fuels.

Types of Solar Energy

PHOTOVOLTAIC

Photovoltaic (PV) technology is a method of converting sunlight directly into electricity using materials that exhibit the photovoltaic effect. This process is the foundation of solar panels and is widely used for generating clean, renewable energy. Converts light into electricity. This is done by using solar panels to capture the sun's rays and create an electric current.

SOLAR THERMAL

Solar thermal technology uses the sun's heat to generate energy, either by directly heating a substance (like water or air) or through a more advanced process where heat is converted into electricity. Unlike photovoltaic systems that convert light into electricity, solar thermal systems focus on capturing and using thermal (heat) energy from the sun. Captures heat from the sun's radiation and converts it into thermal energy. This heat can be used to heat water or air.

CONCENTRATED SOLAR POWER

Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) is a technology that uses mirrors or lenses to concentrate a large area of sunlight onto a small focal point to generate heat. This heat is then used to produce steam, which drives turbines connected to generators to produce electricity. CSP is mainly used in large-scale power plants to provide renewable energy to the grid.

PASSIVE SOLAR

Passive solar refers to the use of the sun's energy for heating and cooling purposes without relying on mechanical devices, such as pumps or fans. Instead, passive solar systems are designed to capture, store, and distribute sunlight naturally through the building's architecture and materials. Uses the sun's energy without mechanical devices. For example, using south-facing windows to provide natural light and heat for a home.
